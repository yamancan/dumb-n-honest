How to read this

Usage is the number of answered human turns. The large number is correction acknowledgments per 100 turns; zero acknowledgments can coexist with nonzero usage.

Black marks explicit ownership of an error. Orange marks explicit acceptance of a correction. Each answered human turn counts once; the total combines both.

All visible bars use the same scale. The default shows the latest three numeric version numbers observed per provider, with at most three versions per family. Include older generations to reveal earlier models. Version numbers are not release dates.

Low sample means fewer than 100 answered turns. The sampling labels use the audit’s event and turn counts: exploratory below 500 turns or 10 events; sample-limited at 10–19 events; sample-sufficient at 20 or more.

95% confidence intervals describe turn-level uncertainty. Tasks, dates, effort and user behavior can differ, and turns may be clustered. A higher rate does not prove a model makes more mistakes.
